How to Use

Everything you need to know to enjoy Moon's Open Playground.

01

👒 Interact with Luffy

Tap directly on Luffy to interact with him. He can react differently depending on what you're doing.

👆 Try tapping him a few times!
02

🍖 Feed Luffy

Luffy gets hungry over time. Keep an eye on the hunger meter in the Captain Panel.

If you have meat in your inventory, press the 🍖 MEAT button to feed him.

🍖 Don't let the captain go hungry!
03

💰 Manage Your Berries

Your Berries are displayed at the top-right of the screen.

💰 100

You'll need Berries when buying items from the Store.

04

🏪 Visit the Store

Open the Store to see items available for Luffy.

Check an item's price and press BUY if you have enough Berries.

🛒 More items may be added in future versions.
05

🎁 Claim Your Daily Treasure

Open the Daily Treasure section to claim your daily reward.

🎁 +50 Berries

The reward becomes available again after 24 hours.

06

⚙️ Open Settings

The 🌙 button opens the Settings menu.

🏠 Home
❓ Help and support
📖 How to use
💬 Support

Use these options to move between the different pages.

07

📱 Mobile Controls

Moon's Open Playground is designed to work on mobile devices as well as desktop browsers.

  • 👆 Tap buttons normally.
  • 👒 Tap Luffy directly to interact.
  • ↕️ Scroll the page to explore more sections.
08

💾 Your Progress

Some information is saved locally in your browser, such as your Berries and meat inventory.

⚠️ Clearing your browser's site data may reset your saved progress.
